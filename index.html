BRYCER 2026 Email Roadmap

Turning Reports into Results — a year-long cadence designed to reduce lack of compliance with clear weekly execution by audience.
Layout
Audience
Prospecting: Weeks 1–9 Drips: Weeks 11–12 Newsletter: Week 13

How this reduces lack of compliance

  • Prospecting creates new programs and modernization momentum.
  • Upsells expand what’s tracked (FE + RR) so fewer items fall through the cracks.
  • Service provider touches improve submission quality and turnaround time.
  • Drips convert by intent (Cold, Warm, High Intent) at the end of the quarter.

Locked cadence rules

  • Weeks 1–9: Prospecting
  • Week 10: Pause new prospect sends (optimize, resend winners)
  • Week 11–12: Drips (Cold / Warm / High Intent)
  • Week 13: Quarterly Newsletter
