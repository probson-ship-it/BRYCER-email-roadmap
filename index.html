Segmentation Frame
How every box on this calendar is targeted

Prospect outreach is filtered through region, population, and quarterly theme. Existing partner promotions run bi-monthly. Quarterly newsletters go to Fire and Backflow accounts.

Prospect regions – South · Midwest · West · Northeast · Mountain · Plains
Population tiers – <25k · 25–50k · 50–250k · 250k+
Fire themes – Q1 Visibility · Q2 Efficiency · Q3 Compliance · Q4 Modernization
Backflow themes – Q1 Visibility · Q2 Program Control · Q3 Compliance Outcomes · Q4 Modernization & Planning
Partner promos – Bi-monthly rotation: Report Review ↔ Fire Extinguishers
