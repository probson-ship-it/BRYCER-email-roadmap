BRYCER 2026 Email Roadmap

Turning Reports into Results — a year-long cadence built to reduce lack of compliance with clear weekly execution by audience.
Prospecting: Weeks 1–9 Week 10: Fire pause + Backflow Region 5 Drips: Weeks 11–12 Newsletter: Week 13

Legend

Fire Prospects (Tue)
Backflow Prospects (Tue)
Upsells (Thu) — FE monthly + RR monthly
Resend (Unopens) — 3–4 business days after (no webinar/newsletter)
Service Providers (1st Wed monthly)
Drips (Week 11–12)
Quarterly Newsletter (Week 13)

Quarter Rules (Locked)

  • Weeks 1–9: Prospecting (Fire weekly; Backflow biweekly by region plan)
  • Week 10: Fire pause (optimize, resend winners, list hygiene) + Backflow Region 5 send
  • Weeks 11–12: Behavior-based drips (Cold / Warm / High Intent)
  • Week 13: Quarterly Newsletter (end of quarter)
  • Resends: Every send except webinars/newsletters gets a resend to unopens 3–4 business days later
