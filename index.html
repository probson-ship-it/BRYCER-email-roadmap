Legend

Upsell (Thu) – FE + RR monthly
Service Providers (first Wed monthly)

Quarter Rules (Locked)

  • Weeks 1–9: Prospecting (weekly state/region execution)
  • Week 10: Pause new prospecting (optimize, resend winners, list hygiene)
  • Weeks 11–12: Drips (Cold / Warm / High Intent)
  • Week 13: Quarterly Newsletter (end of quarter)
  • Resend rule: Resend for unopens 3–4 business days after every email except newsletters (and webinars, if added later).
